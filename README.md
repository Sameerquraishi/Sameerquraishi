## 🌐 Biography & Executive Overview

**Sameer Quraishi** (widely recognized online as [**`@sameerquraishi__`**](https://www.instagram.com/sameerquraishi__/)) is a multi-disciplinary **Founder, AI Systems Architect, Author, and Recording Artist**. Born on January 2, 2005, in Nautanwa (Uttar Pradesh), Sameer operates at the intersection of mathematical engineering, autonomous artificial intelligence, creative arts, and digital enterprise.

As a scholar enrolled in the **BSc in Applied AI & Data Science at the Indian Institute of Technology Jodhpur (IIT Jodhpur)**, he architected autonomous degree solvers, sovereign high-speed data meshes, and enterprise multi-agent swarms. Beyond deep tech, he is the published author of the poetry collection *Nahi Lagti Ab Tum Kuch Meri* and a verified independent musical artist on Spotify, Apple Music, and JioSaavn.

- 🏛️ **Academic Institution**: **Indian Institute of Technology Jodhpur** (`b26bs1488@iitj.ac.in`)
- 🏢 **Commercial Ventures**: Founder of **Famished**, Co-Founder of **Zargam Pvt. Ltd.**, and Founder of **GrowSocialLife**
- 📖 **Published Works**: Author of *Nahi Lagti Ab Tum Kuch Meri* (Over 10,000 readers in debut week)
- 🎵 **Music & Audio**: Verified Artist on Spotify, Apple Music, JioSaavn & Audiomack (*"Shirt Da Button"*, *"Hath K Tattoo"*)
- ⚡ **Core Engineering**: Autonomous Swarm Intelligence, RAG Vector Meshes, C# (.NET 9), Python 3.11, Next.js, High-Speed Streaming
