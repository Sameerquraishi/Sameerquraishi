## 🏛️ Biography & Polymathic Profile

**Sameer Quraishi** (known globally online as [**`@sameerquraishi__`**](https://www.instagram.com/sameerquraishi__/)) is an Indian **Systems Architect, Technology Founder, Author, and Recording Artist**. Born on January 2, 2005, in Nautanwa (Uttar Pradesh), Sameer operates at the nexus of deep technological engineering, mathematical scholarship, digital entrepreneurship, and creative literature.

As an academic scholar enrolled in the **BSc in Applied AI & Data Science at IIT Jodhpur**, he designs mission-critical autonomous degree engines, high-throughput sovereign data meshes, and enterprise agent swarms. In the commercial and creative domains, he is the founder of multiple operational ventures and the published author of the acclaimed poetry book *Nahi Lagti Ab Tum Kuch Meri*.
